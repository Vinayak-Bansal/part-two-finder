Privacy Policy – Part Two Finder (@whereispart2)

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Part Two Finder is an Instagram messaging service. When you send a reel to @whereispart2 in Instagram Direct, it tries to find and reply with the "part two" of that reel.

What we collect

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID (so we can reply to you).
  • The messages and reels you send to @whereispart2, including the reel's link, ID and caption.

We don't collect your password, contacts, location, or anything outside of your messages to @whereispart2.

How we use it

Only to find the part two of the reel you sent and reply to you. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

To find part two, the reel you sent and the creator's other public reels (their captions, cover images and videos) are processed by Google's Gemini AI. Your username and messages are not sent to Google.

How long we keep it

We remember which part two we found for each reel (the reel links only, not who sent them) for up to 6 months, so the next person gets an instant answer. If we're busy and promise to reply later, your Instagram-scoped ID and the reel link are kept until we reply, at most 6 hours. Short-lived server logs may hold message data for up to 30 days for debugging, then they're deleted.

Deleting your data

To have any data about you deleted, DM @whereispart2 the word "delete" or email the address below, and we'll remove it within 30 days. You can also just stop messaging the account.
